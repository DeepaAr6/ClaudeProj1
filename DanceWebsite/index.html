Our Dance Forms

Three wonderful worlds of dance. Which one will you try first?

🕺

Western Dance

Groove to the latest beats! Our western classes are full of energy, style and plenty of fun. Perfect if you love music videos and want to bust some moves.

  • Hip-Hop
  • Contemporary
  • Jazz
  • Salsa

Levels: Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced

🪘

Folk Dance

Celebrate culture with colourful, joyful folk dances from across India. Great for festivals, weddings, and anyone who loves to dance together as a group!

  • Bhangra
  • Garba & Dandiya
  • Lavani
  • Ghoomar

Levels: Beginner · Intermediate

🪷

Classical Dance

Discover the grace, storytelling and expressions of Indian classical dance. Our gentle, step-by-step approach makes these beautiful traditions easy to start.

  • Bharatanatyam
  • Kathak
  • Odissi
  • Kuchipudi

Levels: Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced
