FAQ

Questions, Answered

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first 30-minute meeting is free and comes with no obligation. We'll only quote a fee if you'd like us to prepare a written strategy.

How do you charge for advice?

We charge a fixed fee agreed upfront, never a percentage of your assets and never commissions from product providers. You'll know the cost before any work begins.

Do I need a lot of money to work with you?

No. We help people at every stage, from first-home buyers and young professionals to retirees. Good advice early often makes the biggest difference.

Can we meet online?

Absolutely. We meet clients in our Sydney office, by phone or by video call, whichever suits you best.

What should I prepare for the first meeting?

Nothing formal. A rough idea of your income, savings, super, debts and goals is plenty. We'll guide you through the rest.
